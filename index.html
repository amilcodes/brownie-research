A DIFFERENT DIMENSION OF THINKING.

Brownie Research is a student-led foundation model research lab.

We’re drawn to the questions beneath the surface: how intelligence takes shape, what models understand, and how to study them with care.

Our starting point is simple. Bring curious people together, give ideas room to develop, and do the work to understand them.
